Frequently Asked Questions

  • Skyline Digital Solutions creates clean, professional websites for businesses that want to establish a stronger online presence, build trust with customers, and make it easier for people to contact them.

  • We offer three monthly website plans: Starter, Professional, and Premium.

    Each plan includes custom website design and development, mobile-first design, website content setup, domain and hosting, basic SEO setup, and ongoing website maintenance.

  • Every website plan includes:

    • Fully customized website design and development
    • Fully responsive mobile-first design
    • Website content setup
    • Domain and hosting included
    • Basic SEO setup
    • Ongoing website maintenance
    • Website edits based on your plan
    • A website built from scratch using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript

  • Our monthly plans are:

    • Starter: $199/month
    • Professional: $299/month
    • Premium: $399/month

    Each plan includes a different number of pages and monthly website edits.

  • Yes. Yearly payment options are available for businesses that prefer to pay annually instead of monthly.

    Our yearly plans are:

    • Starter: $1,999/year
    • Professional: $2,999/year
    • Premium: $3,999/year

    Annual plans provide savings compared to paying month-to-month.

  • Yes. All website plans require a signed contract and a 12-month agreement.

    This allows us to build, launch, maintain, and support your website properly while keeping the monthly pricing simple and predictable.

  • No. Because your website is custom designed and developed upfront, the agreement does not allow cancellation before the 12-month term is complete.

    All terms are clearly explained before the project begins.

  • The number of pages depends on your plan:

    • Starter: Up to 3 pages
    • Professional: Up to 5 pages
    • Premium: Up to 5 pages

    Additional pages beyond the included amount are available for $100 per page.

  • Each plan includes a set number of monthly website edits:

    • Starter: 1 edits per month
    • Professional: 5 edits per month
    • Premium: 10 edits per month

    This helps keep your website updated, accurate, and professional over time.

    Additional edits beyond the included amount are available for $50 per edit.

  • A website edit includes any type of modification or update to your website. This can include text changes, image updates, layout adjustments, section changes, content updates, or other website modifications.

    Additional pages are not included as regular edits and are priced separately.

  • Yes. Domain and hosting are included in the monthly price, so you do not have to manage those details separately.

    We help keep the setup simple so your website can stay live, connected, and ready for visitors.

  • Yes. We help create and organize your website content based on the information you provide about your business, services, goals, and customers.

  • Basic SEO setup is included with every plan.

    This includes things like page structure, headings, titles, descriptions, and basic optimization to help your website have a stronger foundation. Advanced SEO, blogging, paid ads, and ongoing marketing are not included.

  • The timeline depends on the size of the project, how many pages are needed, and how quickly the required information is provided.

    After you complete the project questionnaire, we review your details and discuss the expected timeline during the discovery call.

  • Getting started is simple. Click the "Get Started” button and complete the project questionnaire.

    After that, we review your answers, schedule a discovery call, confirm the project details, complete the agreement, and begin building your website.
