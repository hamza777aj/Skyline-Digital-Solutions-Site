Website Plans & Pricing

  • Starter is $499 per month or $4,999 paid annually, with up to five pages in the initial website build and 60 minutes of routine editing each month after launch.

    Professional is $799 per month or $7,999 paid annually, with up to eight initial pages and 120 minutes of routine editing each month after launch.

    Custom includes individually quoted pricing, page counts, features, and monthly editing time.

    Your proposal identifies the pages included in your initial build. Choosing fewer pages does not reduce the plan price or reserve free pages for later. Pages added after the initial approved scope are priced separately, as explained in Question 9.

  • All plans begin with a 12-month commitment starting when the agreement is signed. There is no separate plan setup fee.

    For Starter and Professional, monthly payments begin at signing and recur automatically each month, including during development. Annual payment covers the year upfront and is also due at signing. Custom payment arrangements are documented individually.

    Development starts according to the agreed schedule once the agreement is signed, payment is received, and essential materials are supplied. Because the commitment starts at signing, it includes development time.

    Your billing anniversary is based on the signing date. If that date does not exist in a shorter month, billing moves to month-end and returns to the original date when available. Prices are in USD. Applicable taxes are disclosed before payment and charged separately.

Design & Development

  • We begin with a complimentary consultation to understand your business, project requirements, and goals. After reviewing your needs, we provide a proposal outlining the project scope, pricing, and estimated development timeline.

    Once approved, we design and develop your website, incorporate feedback within the agreed revision process, and complete technical checks before launch. Timelines vary depending on project complexity, required materials, feedback, and approvals. We communicate significant schedule changes and provide updated estimates when necessary.

  • Every plan includes setting up, organizing, and making basic refinements to text you provide. Original copywriting, substantial rewrites, and specialized writing are scoped separately and may cost extra. Any additional deliverables and charges are documented in your approved proposal before development begins.

    During planning, we identify the business information, branding, service details, text, images, and other materials you need to provide.

  • Every plan includes two consolidated revision rounds and one final finishing revision before launch. The first two rounds cover design, layout, and content within the approved scope. The final revision addresses smaller details, such as spacing, typography, and minor content changes.

    Submit one combined feedback list for each round. Work outside the approved scope may cost extra and requires approval. Corrections to errors we cause do not count toward your revision allowance.

Ownership & Handover

  • You own your domain and pay its registration and renewal fees separately. You retain ownership of your original supplied text, images, logos, other materials, and business data. We manage domain configuration on your behalf.

    Skyline retains ownership of its original website designs, source code, development files, written content, and original creative contributions to revised content. Your original supplied materials remain yours. Skyline-owned materials are licensed for use only during your active subscription and cannot be taken to another website afterward. Paying separately for Skyline-created copywriting does not transfer its ownership or extend that license.

    We do not offer buyouts or ownership transfers of Skyline-owned website materials. Third-party materials remain subject to their own licenses. Your domain and client-owned third-party accounts remain under your control when you leave.

  • On the effective termination date, hosting, maintenance, support, and your license to use Skyline-owned website materials end, and the website is taken offline. Website-dependent editing, added-page, and managed-integration services also end automatically and do not renew afterward.

    For voluntary cancellation, current editing-cycle and integration-period charges remain nonrefundable, even if those paid periods extend beyond the website’s termination date. Added-page minimums and refunds follow Question 9. Qualifying termination for a material service failure follows Question 20. You remain responsible for separately canceling subscriptions held directly with third-party providers.

    Within 10 business days after effective termination, we provide your original supplied materials and relevant business data at no additional charge. Files are delivered by email or suitable file transfer in available original or appropriate export formats. Skyline-owned materials are excluded. Standard handover does not include rebuilding the website or importing materials into another provider’s platform.

  • We retain working copies of client-supplied materials and relevant business data for up to 60 calendar days after effective termination. During that period, you can request help with missing files or problems accessing the materials delivered in your standard handover, at no additional charge.

    We delete ordinary working copies at the end of that period. Backup copies follow their separate retention schedules, and records needed for legal requirements or legitimate business obligations may be retained longer. Handover assistance does not include ongoing migration support or new development work.

Report the concern in writing to the same address, identifying the agreed service and the problem. We provide a substantive response within five business days. A material failure substantially affects delivery or use of an agreed service. A missed estimated date alone, a minor defect, or a request outside the agreed scope does not automatically qualify. Your agreement defines the qualifying circumstances and applicable adjustments.

Questions 19 and 20 explain applicable charges and refunds. The separate material-service-failure procedure in Question 17 does not require this additional notice period.
